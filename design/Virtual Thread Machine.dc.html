KEY BEHAVIORS
1 · Mount
Runnable VT is mounted on a free carrier thread.
2 · Park
Blocking I/O unmounts the VT; its continuation is stored on the heap and the carrier is released.
3 · Resume
I/O completes; the VT re-enters the queue and remounts on any carrier.
4 · Pinned
Blocking inside synchronized/native code pins the carrier — it cannot be released.
